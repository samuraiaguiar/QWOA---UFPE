Supplementary CSV files for the QWOA portfolio manuscript.

aggregate_summary.csv: 177-row aggregate table grouped by method, n, k, and depth.
best_per_group.csv: best optimizer/penalty choice per instance, method, and depth.
extended_results_df.csv: raw optimizer runs.
equivalence_checks.csv: complete-graph/Grover equivalence checks.
resource_estimates_df.csv and circuit_resource_df.csv: graph- and circuit-level resource estimates.
Other CSV files contain market data and intermediate tables used by the notebook.
